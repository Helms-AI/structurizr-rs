# FreshMart Loyalty & Rewards Architecture

## Overview

The FreshMart Loyalty & Rewards Platform manages 25M+ members, processes 500M+ points daily, and drives 60% of company revenue through personalized engagement.

## Key Capabilities

### Customer Engagement
- **25M+ Members**: Unified customer profiles
- **Customer 360**: Aggregated customer view
- **ML Personalization**: Real-time recommendations
- **Multi-tier Membership**: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum

### Points Engine
- **500M+ Points/Day**: Earned and redeemed
- **Real-time Processing**: Sub-second point updates
- **Flexible Rules**: Base, bonus, promotional earning
- **85% Redemption Rate**: High engagement

### Personalization
- **15+ ML Models**: Recommendations, segmentation, propensity
- **<100ms Inference**: Real-time personalization
- **Neural CF**: Deep learning recommendations
- **Churn Prediction**: Proactive retention

### Gamification
- **100+ Challenges**: Engagement mechanics
- **50+ Badges**: Achievement system
- **Leaderboards**: Social competition
- **Bonus Points**: Challenge rewards

## Technology Stack

- **ML**: TensorFlow, XGBoost, Neural CF
- **Backend**: Java/Spring Boot, Node.js
- **Databases**: PostgreSQL, ScyllaDB, Redis
- **Messaging**: Apache Kafka, Firebase
- **APIs**: REST, GraphQL

## Business Impact

- 60% of revenue from members
- 85% redemption rate
- Real-time personalization
- Multi-channel engagement
